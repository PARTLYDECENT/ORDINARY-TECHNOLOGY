Desktop Recycling Facts

🖥️ E-waste is the fastest growing waste stream in the world, with over 50 million tons generated annually.
💻 A desktop computer typically contains valuable materials including gold, silver, aluminum, and copper that can be recovered.
🔋 Many computer components contain harmful materials like lead, mercury, and cadmium that can contaminate soil and water if improperly disposed.
♻️ Recycling one million laptops saves energy equivalent to electricity used by 3,657 homes for a year.
📊 Only about 20% of e-waste is formally recycled, with the remainder often ending up in landfills.
⚡ Manufacturing a new computer takes at least 10 times more energy than refurbishing an existing one.
🌱 Many manufacturers now offer take-back programs for recycling old equipment.
🔒 Always remember to wipe your data before recycling any computer equipment.
