The Composite Unistrut Concept

Why Fiberglass? Standard 1-5/8" unistrut is highly convenient to bolt together, but flexes significantly under heavy cargo.

By wrapping unistrut elements with epoxy-impregnated glass-fiber sleeves, the composite shell absorbs the extreme tension forces along the bottom edges, while the steel core absorbs compression. This creates an incredibly rigid chassis with negligible extra weight!
