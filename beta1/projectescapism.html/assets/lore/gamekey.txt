[THE BACKSTORY (Project HGT-109)]
Space Force harvested threat compound HGT-109 to 
control human hosts. Instead, HGT-109 acted as a 
quantum receiver, syncing host DNA to the non-local 
"Hive Soul" network. This caused host death and post
-mortem re-animation into infectious shambling vectors 
the Horde).

[CAMPAIGN TIMELINE]
1. DEPLOY: Control Chassis T-1997 (Special Unit 76) in Sector Delta.
2. REGEN: Retrieve Fuel Cell, Ionizer, and Scoop Core (marked by cyan beacons) to build the Hydrogen Scoop (+10 HP/sec regen).
3. PURGE: Neutralize three armored Swarm Hive Nodes (500 HP each) that spawn endless waves of mutants.
4. FOLD: Final node purge triggers orbital warp (Fold Protocol) to Level 2: Desolation.
