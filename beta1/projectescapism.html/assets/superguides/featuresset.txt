Here is the breakthrough feature set and key technological highlights of Mars Entropy, compiled directly from the codebase and documentation:

Core Gameplay & Narrative
Voxel Survival x Cosmic Horror Loop: Mine raw minerals (Hematite, Sulfur, Magma Veins) and construct bases (Habitat, Power Generators, Laser Towers) while monitoring mechanical vitals: Health, Oxygen, Lubrication, Energy, and Power.
Psychotic Dual-AI Director System: Experience a fractured cortex with competing narrators in 
Narrator
—the male "System" trying to guide you through objectives, and "Maria," a glitched entity who breaks the fourth wall, aware she is inside a computer simulation.
Reality-Bending Entropy States: Trigger the "Unstable World" phase where the sky bleeds red, gravity inverts, voxel structures dissolve, and players can visit a physical "Blue Screen of Death" dimension.
Campaign Progression: Play through a 6-level story flow across diverse environments, including Mars Crash Site, Underground Labs, battlefield war zones, and liminal maze backrooms.
Advanced Gameplay Systems
Dynamic Planetary Rover: Drive a modular vehicle using 
Rover

Real-Time Strategy (RTS) Mode: Switch to an isometric perspective with 
RTSManager

Physics Gravity Gun: Utilize 
PhysicsGun

Coplanar Alignment Puzzles: Solve 3D environmental puzzles with 
AncientKeystone

Voxel Painting & Splattering: Toggle paint mode via 
PaintSystem

CRT Holographic Fast Travel: Access unlocked story checkpoints and coordinates across 10 sandbox worlds through 
FastTravel

Comprehensive Admin & Creator Panel: Toggle 
AdminMenu

AAA Corner-Expanding Damage Grid: A custom GLSL shader overlays visual diagnostic grids, circuit lines, data streams, and glowing corruption blocks that dynamically grow from the corner of the screen as structural integrity drops.
Medusa Inverse Kinematics: Face enemies featuring procedurally animated 16-tentacle spines that dynamically sway, reach, and snap based on surrounding geometry.
Tri-Planar Voxel Meshing: Custom WebGL 2.0 rendering featuring greedy meshing, chunk culling, and tri-planar PBR material mapping to eliminate texture stretching on voxel terrain slopes.

SaveSystem
